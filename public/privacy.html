Skin preview privacy notice

Last updated July 11, 2026

The short version: we use your photos to create this preview. The app does not intentionally save your photos or result. If your photos pass the local capture checks, they are sent to Google Gemini for AI processing, and Google's data rules may also apply.

What the preview processes

  • The one or three photos you submit.
  • The body area you select.
  • Basic technical information needed for security and reliable operation, such as your network address, request timing, and error details.

We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

How photos are used

We use your photos only to check image quality and describe visible surface features. We do not use them to identify you, estimate your age, make a medical diagnosis, or decide whether a treatment is right for you.

Application storage

The app processes photos during your request and does not intentionally save the photos or result in app storage. Closing the page clears the result from your browser. System logs are designed not to include photos, result text, or contact information.

AI processing

If your photos pass the app's local capture checks, the app sends them to Google Gemini to create the preview. Google's applicable API data and retention rules may apply. We do not promise zero provider retention unless we have confirmed it for the account in use.

Your choice

Uploading photos is optional. Do not continue if you do not want Von & Co and Google Gemini to process them. This preview is for guests age 18 or older.

Medical limitation

This is not a medical diagnosis. Do not use it to evaluate a mole, changing spot, rash, wound, infection, or other medical concern. Please contact a qualified healthcare professional.

Questions

For questions about this notice, use the contact options on the Von & Co website or call 239.799.4866.

Skin preview privacy notice

Last updated July 11, 2026

The short version: your photos are used to create the visible skin preview. The Von & Co application does not intentionally save the photos or result to a database or file. The photos are sent to a configured AI provider for processing, so provider-side data handling also applies.

What the preview processes

  • One or three photographs that you choose to submit.
  • The body area selected for the preview.
  • Limited technical information needed for security, rate limiting, and reliable operation, such as network address, request timing, and error details.

The preview does not request your name, email address, or phone number.

How photos are used

Photos are processed only to assess whether the capture is usable and to describe visible surface features. The application does not use them to identify you, diagnose a condition, estimate biological age, or determine whether a treatment is medically appropriate.

Application storage

The Von & Co application processes uploaded photos in memory during the request and does not intentionally write the photos or result to persistent application storage. Closing the page clears the result from the page. Operational logs are designed not to contain photos, analysis text, or contact information.

AI provider processing

Depending on availability and the configured account, the preview may use OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic to process the photographs. Provider-side handling and retention depend on the terms and data controls active for the Von & Co account. Von & Co will not claim zero provider retention unless that setting has been verified for the active account.

Your choice

Submitting a photo is optional. Do not upload a photo if you do not want it processed by Von & Co and the configured AI provider. The tool is available only to guests who confirm that they are at least 18 years old.

Medical limitation

This preview cannot diagnose or rule out skin disease. Do not rely on it for a mole, changing lesion, rash, wound, infection, or other medical concern. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical evaluation.

Questions
